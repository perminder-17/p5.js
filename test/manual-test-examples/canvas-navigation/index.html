Canvas navigation

One tab stop for the whole canvas, arrow keys inside it, Escape to leave. Turn on a screen reader and Tab into the sketch, or watch the log on the right to see what it would say.

← Tab from here. The canvas is the next stop, and the button below is the one after that.
← one Tab away, however many items the sketch drew.

Controls

Turn on add 400 points and arrow to the end of the list: the tree stops enumerating and gives one summary item instead. Turn off animating to confirm the tree and the ring still work with no draw loop running.

Keys

Tabreach the canvas, or leave it ↓next item ↑previous item, or back to the summary →open an item for detail, or step into a group ←close an item, or go back to its group Enterpress a button drawn on the canvas Escback to the summary

What a screen reader says

Nothing yet. The canvas stays silent until it is asked.

Focus probe

idle

Reads document.activeElement live. If it names an item inside the canvas, this browser puts canvas fallback content in the focus order. If it stays on the canvas itself, navigation still works through aria-activedescendant.
